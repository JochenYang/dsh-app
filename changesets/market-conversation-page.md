插件市场改为「会话区页面」形态：入口仍在侧边栏底部，点开后页面**精确覆盖会话区域**（侧边栏保持可见），窗口缩放、内容滚动时实时跟随；会话关闭或进入无会话界面时页面自动收起，Esc 也可关闭。数据与安装链路不变。
The plugin market becomes a conversation-area page: the entry stays in the sidebar foot, and opening it covers exactly the conversation column (the sidebar remains visible), tracking window resizes and scrolls live; closing a session or leaving the conversation collapses the page automatically, and Escape closes too. Data and install flows are unchanged.
