启动页页脚那句温暖话现在从十句里每次启动随机取一句：原有的「首先您要健康，其次才是其次」与「想要的太多，需要的不多」，另加「慢一点，路才看得见」「不必赢过谁，走完自己的路就好」「把今天过好，明天自会接上」「真正的自由，是能说不」「心里有数，就不必声张」「与其追赶时间，不如把此刻做完」「够用，就是富足」「允许自己休息，也是一种努力」；同一次启动内不再变化，起几次才可能换到别的句子（十句集中在外壳的一处常量，以后加句仍只改这一行）。
The wish line at the foot of the splash is now one of ten, drawn at random once per app start: the two it already had ("Stay healthy first. Everything else comes second.", "We want so much more than we need.") plus eight new ones ("Slow down, and the road appears.", "You need not outrun anyone — just finish your road.", "Live today well, and tomorrow follows.", "Real freedom is being able to say no.", "Knowing your own measure, you need not announce it.", "Rather than chase time, finish this moment.", "Enough is abundance.", "Resting is also a kind of effort."). It does not change while that start runs, only across starts, and the ten live in one shell constant, so adding a wish is still one line.
